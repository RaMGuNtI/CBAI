Acme Innovations — Product Specification and Warranty Guide

1. Acme UltraBot X1
The Acme UltraBot X1 is an autonomous AI workstation designed for edge computing and continuous telemetry data processing.
- Battery Life: 18 hours of continuous operation on standard 120V power adapter.
- Operating Temperature: -10 degrees Celsius to 50 degrees Celsius.
- Storage: 2TB NVMe PCIe 4.0 SSD with hardware-level AES-256 encryption.
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, Gigabit Ethernet, and dual USB-C Thunderbolt 4 ports.

2. Warranty and Return Policies
- Return Window: Customers may return any undamaged Acme hardware within 30 calendar days of delivery for a 100% full refund.
- Manufacturer Warranty: Every new device includes a 2-year limited warranty covering mechanical and electrical component defects.
- Accidental Damage: Accidental liquid spills and physical drops are covered under the optional AcmeCare+ extended plan.
- RMA Process: To initiate a return, contact support@acme-innovations.com with your serial number and invoice.

3. Maintenance and Care
- Clean exterior surfaces exclusively with isopropyl alcohol (70%) and a microfiber cloth.
- Never submerge any component in water or expose the vents to pressurized steam.
- Firmware updates are deployed over-the-air (OTA) on the first Tuesday of each month at 02:00 UTC.
